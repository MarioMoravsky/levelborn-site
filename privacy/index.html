This Privacy Policy explains how Levelborn processes information when you use the Levelborn mobile application.

1. Who operates Levelborn?

2. What is Levelborn?

Levelborn is a gamified habit and personal progress application. It allows users to create quests and routines, earn experience points, build streaks, increase skills, unlock achievements and rewards, participate in challenges, customize a player profile and optionally synchronize progress through a Levelborn account.

3. Information we process

Account information

If you create a Levelborn account, we may process:

  • Your email address
  • Your internal user/account identifier
  • Authentication and session information
  • Your player name
  • Your avatar and profile settings

Authentication and cloud infrastructure are provided using Supabase.

Progress and application data

Depending on the features you use, Levelborn may process:

  • Quests and habits you create
  • Quest schedules
  • Reminder settings
  • Quest completion history
  • XP and player level
  • Ranks and skill progress
  • Streaks
  • Achievements
  • Weekly boss and challenge progress
  • Unlocked rewards and cosmetics
  • Selected avatar and profile customization
  • Application settings

Some information is stored locally on your device. When you use a Levelborn account and cloud functionality, relevant information may also be stored in Levelborn's Supabase-backed cloud database.

Purchase and subscription information

Levelborn may offer optional paid features through Levelborn PRO.

Payments are processed by the applicable app store, including Google Play and, where available, the Apple App Store.

Levelborn uses RevenueCat to manage purchase status and premium entitlements.

Information processed in connection with purchases may include:

  • Whether Levelborn PRO is active
  • The product or subscription purchased
  • Purchase and subscription status
  • Transaction and entitlement identifiers
  • Renewal or expiration status
  • Cancellation status
  • Purchase restoration information

Levelborn does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card details.

Notifications

If you enable quest reminders, Levelborn may schedule local notifications on your device.

Notification permissions and reminder settings are used to provide the reminder functionality you request.

Technical information

Levelborn and its service providers may process limited technical information required for authentication, cloud synchronization, purchase verification, security and service reliability.

This may include:

  • Application identifiers
  • Device or operating system information
  • IP address
  • Timestamps
  • Authentication/session information
  • Diagnostic or service request information

4. Why we process information

We process information where necessary to:

  • Provide Levelborn features
  • Create and authenticate accounts
  • Synchronize your progress between devices
  • Store quests and progression
  • Provide reminders you enable
  • Verify and restore Levelborn PRO purchases
  • Provide customer support
  • Protect accounts and prevent abuse
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the service
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Apple App Store

6. Local storage and cloud storage

Some Levelborn information may be stored locally on your device.

Where cloud synchronization is enabled, relevant information may also be stored in Levelborn's cloud infrastructure.

Deleting the Levelborn app from your device does not necessarily delete your Levelborn cloud account or cloud-stored information.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary:

8. Data retention

Delete your Levelborn account
