Delete Your Levelborn Account

Levelborn gives registered users control over their account and associated cloud data.

Delete your account inside Levelborn

Open the Levelborn app and go to:

Settings → Account → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps shown in the application.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What will be deleted?

When your Levelborn account is permanently deleted, Levelborn will delete or anonymize account-related cloud information under our control, including where applicable:

  • Levelborn account and profile information
  • Cloud-synchronized quests
  • Quest schedules
  • Quest completion history
  • XP, level and rank information
  • Skills and streaks
  • Achievements
  • Weekly boss and challenge progress
  • Rewards and customization settings
  • Other cloud progress linked to the account

Limited information may be retained where necessary for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or security purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Levelborn account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or Apple App Store subscription.

If you have an active Levelborn PRO subscription, you must manage or cancel it through the store where it was purchased.

Google, Apple, RevenueCat and other service providers may also retain transaction information according to their own legal and operational requirements.
